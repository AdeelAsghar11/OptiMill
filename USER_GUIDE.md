# OptiMill — User Guide

Welcome to OptiMill, the AI-powered marketplace for CAD manufacturing.

## For Clients
1. **Upload CAD**: Go to the "Analyze" tab and upload your STL file.
2. **AI Analysis**: Wait a few seconds for the AI to classify your design and infer the required materials.
3. **Get Quotes**: Once analyzed, click "Find Shops" to see a ranked list of manufacturers recommended for your specific part.
4. **Compare**: View quotes side-by-side and select the best fit.
5. **Secure Payment**: Pay via Stripe. Your funds are held in escrow until you confirm delivery.

## For Shop Masters
1. **Set Up Shop**: Create your profile, list your machines (CNC, 3D Printer), and the materials you work with.
2. **Receive Requests**: Check your dashboard for incoming quote requests from clients.
3. **Submit Quotes**: Provide a price and estimated delivery time.
4. **Manage Orders**: Track the progress of orders from production to shipping.

## PWA Features
OptiMill is a Progressive Web App. You can install it on your iPhone or Android:
- **iOS**: Tap the "Share" icon in Safari and select "Add to Home Screen".
- **Android**: Tap the "Install" prompt or the three dots menu in Chrome and select "Install App".
